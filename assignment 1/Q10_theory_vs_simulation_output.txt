TASK 10 - THEORY VERSUS SIMULATION
Analytical six-interferer SIR (N=7,n=4): 18.66 dB
Illustrative Monte Carlo mean SIR         : 20.91 dB
Illustrative Monte Carlo 5th percentile   : 18.64 dB

Reasons for differences:
1. Sampled mobile/interferer distances are unequal.
2. Shadowing and antenna patterns change received powers.
3. Finite network boundaries alter the number of interferers.
4. Erlang-B assumes Poisson arrivals and a loss system.
Simulation is a model; it does not replace field measurements.
