TASK 2 - GOVERNING DESIGN PARAMETERS

Fixed cluster reuse
  Governing parameter: N (cluster size)
  Effect: Q=sqrt(3N), channels/cell=floor(336/N)

Sectorised reuse
  Governing parameter: Effective co-channel interferers
  Effect: Sector count, beamwidth, antenna discrimination

Macro-micro deployment
  Governing parameter: Cell radius R and active small cells
  Effect: Cell density is proportional to 1/R^2
